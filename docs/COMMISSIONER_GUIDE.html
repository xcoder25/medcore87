Dear Commissioner,

MedCore OS is Akwa Ibom State's integrated hospital management platform — built to unify every department of a modern hospital into one intelligent system. This guide takes you through the most important features in a simple, step-by-step format. No technical knowledge is needed.

The platform is live at http://localhost:3003 on the demonstration laptop provided by the ICT team.
⚠️
Please ensure the ICT officer has started both terminal windows (the API server and the Hospital OS) before you begin. The top bar of the application should show a green LIVE indicator.

🔐 Step 1 — Signing In

Open the browser and navigate to http://localhost:3003. You will see the MedCore secure login screen.

For your evaluation, use the Medical Director account — this gives you access to every module in the system.

📊 Step 2 — The Hospital Command Centre

Click "Hospital Operations Hub" in the left sidebar. This is the real-time operations dashboard — equivalent to a control room that shows the entire hospital at a glance, live and updated every few seconds.

🛏️
Bed Occupancy
Live count of occupied, available, and beds under cleaning across all wards — ICU, General, Maternity, Paediatrics.
🚨
Emergency Alerts
Critical laboratory results, pharmacy stock-outs, and patient deterioration alerts appear here in real time via the notification bell.
👨‍⚕️
Patient Census
Total inpatients, OPD attendance, discharges today, and average length of stay — all computed automatically.
💰
Revenue Today
Cashier collections, HMO/AKSHIA settlements, and AkwaRemit state revenue — consolidated in one tile.
💡
Click on any metric tile to drill down into a department-level breakdown. For example, clicking "Bed Occupancy" shows which specific wards have availability.

🔍 Step 3 — Clinical Google Search (AI Feature)

"Instead of clicking through 5 years of patient records, a doctor simply types a question — and gets an instant, cited answer in under half a second."

In the sidebar, click "Clinical Google (Search EMR)" under Clinical Tools. This opens MedCore's flagship Generative AI feature — a natural language search bar that reads a patient's entire medical history and synthesizes a precise answer with exact cited sources.

🎯 Demonstration — Try These Queries

1
The patient Mrs Blessing Etim is pre-selected. Her complete 5-year clinical history (laboratory results, medications, and clinic notes) is loaded in the system.
2
In the search bar, type this question exactly:
Summarize this patient's history of hypertension medications and show me their highest creatinine level.
3
Press Enter. Within 0.3 seconds, the system returns:
  • A clear narrative summary of her blood pressure treatment history
  • Her peak creatinine: 186 µmol/L (recorded 14 June 2024)
  • 4 exact clinical citations: the specific lab report, clinic note, and pharmacy record
  • 3 action buttons: Insert into SOAP Note · Order Repeat Creatinine STAT · Audit Regimen vs eGFR
4
Now switch to the Obstetric patient (Mrs Amaka Chukwu) using the patient selector at the top. Type: "What is her gestational blood pressure trajectory and pre-eclampsia screen?"
The system immediately identifies her peak blood pressure of 152/98 mmHg and protein spillage of 420 mg/24 hours — a pre-eclampsia risk flag — all cited from the original ANC visit records.
✅
What this replaces: A doctor previously had to click through 5 different menus, scroll through multiple PDF attachments, and manually calculate the creatinine trend from raw numbers. This now takes one typed question and half a second.

📡 Step 4 — Telehealth Mesh for Remote LGAs

🎯 Demonstration — Test the Mesh

1
Click "Trigger Peer Discovery" — the system simulates all 6 clinics scanning for each other's Wi-Fi signals. The RSSI (signal strength) values update live.
2
Click "Broadcast DTN Bundle" — Essien Udim sends a triage vitals data packet to the mesh. A unique SHA-256 security checksum is generated instantly. The vector clock increments — proving data integrity.
3
Click "Sync via Satellite Uplink" — all queued data from all 6 nodes is flushed up to the state server. A Merkle root (a cryptographic receipt) is generated as proof that all data arrived correctly and in full.
4
Click "Simulate Blackout" — this simulates a complete power/internet outage. All 6 nodes switch to store-and-forward mode. When you click "Restore Connectivity", data automatically re-syncs. No patient records are lost.
💡
USB Sneakernet: For the Creek Boat Floating Clinic where even Wi-Fi is unavailable, a staff member can export all patient records to an encrypted USB drive, physically carry it to the nearest facility, and import it — maintaining a complete and unbroken patient record.

⭐ Step 6 — Other Features Worth Seeing

🤖 Celestia AI Copilot

Click "Celestia" (Brain icon) in the sidebar. Ask it:

  • "What is the current bed occupancy in ICU?"
  • "Show me patients with critical NEWS2 scores today"
  • "Draft a referral letter for a patient to nephrology"

Celestia responds in seconds — streaming its answer like a conversation, with clinical precision.

🧬 LAMIS HIV Clinic & PEPFAR Reporting

Click "LAMIS · NDR & e-CRVS". This module manages Akwa Ibom's HIV treatment programme: ART regimen tracking, viral load monitoring, CD4 counts, and automatic generation of the PEPFAR RADET report that is submitted to the Federal Government. Birth and death registrations (e-CRVS) are also filed from the same screen.

💳 AKSHIA State Insurance Integration

Click "AkwaRemit State Revenue Hub". Every AKSHIA health insurance claim is adjudicated, tracked, and remitted to the state treasury — all from within MedCore OS, with a tamper-proof audit trail.

📋 AI Clinical Automations

Click "AI Clinical Automations". Doctors can dictate to the phone and the AI writes the clinical note. The system automatically checks for dangerous drug-allergy combinations before any prescription is dispensed. Patients whose condition is deteriorating are flagged (NEWS2 Early Warning) before a crisis occurs.

🔒 Compliance & Audit Logs

Every action taken in MedCore OS — every record opened, every medication dispensed, every payment collected — is logged in a tamper-evident audit trail. Click "Clinical Audit & Quality Logs" to see a live feed. This satisfies NHIA, HIPAA, and NDPR requirements.
