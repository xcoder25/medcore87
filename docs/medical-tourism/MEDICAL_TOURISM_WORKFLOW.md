# Medical Tourism & Concierge Workflow

1. **Inquiry & Triage**: Patient submits international inquiry with medical records & origin details.
2. **Clinical Feasibility Review**: Doctor reviews remote imaging and approves travel.
3. **Concierge Coordination**: Flight booking, visa letters, partner hotel accommodations, and transport.
4. **Interpreter Assignment**: Certified medical interpreter paired with patient in their native tongue.
5. **In-Hospital Treatment**: Seamless care flow inside MedCore Clinic and MedCore OS.
6. **Post-Discharge & Repatriation**: Telehealth follow-ups and care navigation back home.
