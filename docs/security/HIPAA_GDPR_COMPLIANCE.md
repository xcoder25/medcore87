# Security & Compliance Framework

- **HIPAA (Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act)**:
  - Cryptographic audit trails for all Protected Health Information (PHI) transactions.
  - End-to-end TLS 1.3 in transit and AES-256 at rest.
  - Fine-grained role-based access control (RBAC).

- **GDPR & International Cross-Border Protection**:
  - Patient consent management for medical tourism records transfers.
  - Right to data portability & right to be forgotten compliance.
